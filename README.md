# Ethical Considerations and Concerns over the Rapid Development of AI

**Nicolas Bustamante** · B.S. Software Development, Champlain College · Course research paper and presentation (SOCI-260, Tech, Society & Culture)

## Overview

AI is changing healthcare, education, law enforcement, software development, and commerce, and it raises ethical concerns that need a solid framework for responsible development. Drawing on the European Commission's *Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI*, UNESCO's *Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*, Hutchens (2023), and Ma, Ong & Tan (2024), this paper looks at:

- **Core principles:** transparency and explainability, accountability, fairness and non-discrimination, and harm prevention
- **Emerging challenges:** algorithmic monoculture, misinformation, global inequality, and human-centric AI development
- **A proposed framework** for ethical AI development that balances innovation with societal well-being
